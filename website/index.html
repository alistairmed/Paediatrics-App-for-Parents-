Questions parents ask

Is Paeds Pouch free?

Yes. The website version is free to use, and the iPhone and Android apps will be free to start. If we add paid extras later, we'll say so clearly before you pay for anything.

Where is my child's information kept?

On your own device. The website version saves records in your browser on that phone or computer, not on our servers, and there's no account. Export a backup file from time to time, because clearing your browser data deletes the records.

Is this medical advice?

No. Paeds Pouch helps you keep records and gives general information based on Australian guidance. It doesn't diagnose or replace your GP, child health nurse or pharmacist. In an emergency, call 000. For advice any time, call 13 HEALTH (13 43 25 84) in Queensland or healthdirect on 1800 022 222.

How is it different from the Blue Book or My Health Record?

Keep using your child's Blue Book (Personal Health Record) and My Health Record. Paeds Pouch sits alongside them: an easy place on your phone to note growth, vaccines, milestones and questions between checks, so you have them ready at appointments.

Who makes Paeds Pouch?

Dr Alistair, a paediatric doctor in Queensland. It's based on Australian guidance such as the National Immunisation Program and the Blue Book checks.

What's the difference between the website version and the apps?

The website version is a free trial you can use now in any browser. The iPhone and Android apps are coming soon; join the waitlist to hear when they're ready.
