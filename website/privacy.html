Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026 · Last updated 15 November 2026

This policy explains what this website (paedspouch.vercel.app) and the Paeds Pouch email newsletter collect, why, where it is kept and your choices. Paeds Pouch is run by Dr Alistair, a paediatric doctor in Queensland. We follow the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Please don't send us your child's health details by email or social media message. We can't give individual medical advice. In an emergency, call 000.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the waitlist or newsletter.
  • Sign-up details our email provider records automatically: the date you joined and confirmed, and the page you signed up on.
  • Email activity: whether our emails are opened and which links are clicked. This helps us see what parents find useful.
  • Anything you choose to send us, for example if you email us or message us on Instagram or Facebook.

This website does not ask for your child's name, date of birth or any health information. It does not use advertising cookies or analytics.

Why we use it

  • To send the confirmation email and your free sheet.
  • To send waitlist news about the Paeds Pouch app, our parent newsletter and news about our printables.
  • To reply to you if you contact us.

We don't sell, rent or trade your details, and we don't use them for anything else without asking you.

Who we share it with

  • Kit (Kit, formerly ConvertKit) stores our email list and sends our emails.
  • Vercel hosts this website. Like any web host it receives technical information when a page loads, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and keeps it in short-term logs for security and reliability.
  • Google Fonts supplies the website's fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta) handle anything you send us there, under Meta's own privacy policy.

Overseas disclosure (Australian Privacy Principle 8). Kit, Vercel, Google and Meta are based in the United States and may store or process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. They are not bound by the Australian Privacy Principles, and Australian law may not give you the same protection or remedies there. By joining the list you agree to your email address being stored with Kit in this way. If you don't agree, please don't sign up.

Unsubscribing

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Once you unsubscribe we stop sending you emails straight away. You can also ask us to delete your details completely by emailing paedspouch@gmail.com.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address while you are subscribed. If you unsubscribe, Kit keeps a record so you aren't emailed again by mistake; we delete it on request.

Keeping it safe

Access to our email list is limited to Paeds Pouch and protected by a password. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your details from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Children

This website and newsletter are for parents and carers aged 18 and over. Please don't sign up if you are under 18.

The Paeds Pouch app

The app, including its website version, has its own Privacy Policy, shown inside the app. In short, your child's records are stored on your device, not on our servers.

Access, correction and complaints

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or make a privacy complaint by emailing paedspouch@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes

If we change how we handle information we'll update this page and the date at the top.
